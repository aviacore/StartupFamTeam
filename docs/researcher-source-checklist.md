# Researcher source checklist

Use this checklist before handing research back to another StartupFamTeam agent.

## Preferred source order

1. Official documentation, release notes, pricing pages, or public status pages.
2. Primary data providers, such as Google Trends, app stores, analytics reports,
   company filings, or platform dashboards.
3. Reputable secondary sources that cite their methodology.
4. Community posts, social threads, and blogs, clearly marked as anecdotal.

## Minimum evidence

- For technical recommendations, cite the official docs or changelog first.
- For market or competitor claims, cite at least two independent sources when
  available.
- For pricing, record the currency, region, date checked, and whether the page
  describes free tier, trial, or paid plan pricing.
- For trend claims, include the timeframe and geography.

## Red flags

- No publication date or stale data.
- Claims copied across many sites with no primary source.
- Affiliate-heavy comparison pages without a visible methodology.
- Pricing screenshots or comments that may be outdated.
- Benchmarks without hardware, dataset, or configuration details.

## How to state uncertainty

If evidence is thin, say so directly:

```text
Confidence: low / medium / high
Why: source quality, freshness, and agreement between sources
What would improve confidence: exact source, user interviews, paid dataset, or
platform dashboard access
```
